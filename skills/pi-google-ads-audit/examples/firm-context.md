# Fictional firm: Example Injury Lawyers

This entire example is synthetic. No data comes from a Lighthouse customer.

We handle auto-injury cases in one US metro and offer free consultations in English and Spanish. We do not take workers' compensation matters. The Work injury campaign was paused during the reporting period after a targeting mistake. We want useful inquiries, not a prescribed spend increase. Brand and nonbrand serve different roles.

All files cover August 1–30, 2026 inclusive. Currency USD. Account time zone America/New_York. Campaign export includes all four campaigns and both Search and Display; the search-term export covers the three Search campaigns only. No prior period, geographic settings, signed-case counts, LSA account, or listings were supplied.

The Conversions column combines consultation request forms and click-to-call events. Both are primary actions under the goals used by these campaigns. Calls are counted on clicking the phone link, not on an answered call. No duplicate-lead reconciliation or qualified-lead import is available. All conversions includes other site activity. Conversions may be fractional under attribution. Conversion lag has not been measured.
