# Example findings — fictional account

This shortened illustration uses the bundled Example Injury Lawyers files. It is not a real firm report, a benchmark, or a claim about results the skill will always find. An actual audit should also include the report template's source appendix and proposed measurement plan.

## First priority

The account spent **$8,000** and recorded **22.5 conversions** during August 1–30, 2026. Those conversions combine consultation forms with phone-link clicks. The calculated **$355.56 per reported conversion** does not establish the cost of an answered call, unique qualified inquiry, or signed case. Agree on useful-inquiry measurement before making a broad allocation decision.

## What the data supports

| Finding | Evidence | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Auto injury is the main allocation question | $6,000 of $8,000 spend; 12.5 reported conversions | Review nonbrand inquiry quality separately from Brand's cheaper, different-intent conversions. |
| Some visible queries fall outside the firm's stated services | Employment query $300; workers' compensation query $600 | Review the narrow query exclusions and confirm the already-paused Work injury campaign's correction. This is $900 of observed spend, not promised savings. |
| The term report is incomplete | $5,050 visible term spend / $7,500 Search spend = 67.3% | The remaining $2,450 is unavailable at query level. It is not automatically irrelevant traffic. Display's $500 is excluded from this denominator. |
| Free-consultation and Spanish queries fit the brief | The firm explicitly offers both; the two rows record 5 conversions on $1,500 | Do not exclude them merely because they contain “free,” are Spanish, or appear under broad matching. Lead quality still needs evidence. |
| Display needs a separate purpose review | $500 spend, zero Conversions, 20 All conversions | Those 20 other events do not establish 20 inquiries. Ask what business objective the campaign is intended to serve. |

## Suggested next steps

The agency's measurement lead and the firm's intake lead should map reported actions to useful inquiries, recording duplicate handling and known lag. The account manager can review a campaign-scoped negative exact-match candidate for `car accident lawyer jobs`, first checking that it will not exclude desired queries. No change is applied by the skill.

The strategist should then assess Auto injury with the firm's outcome data and capacity, while keeping Brand and Display separate. Historical totals reconcile to the campaign file's summary; the paused campaign's spend remains part of the period. There is no supplied prior period, signed-case data, location configuration, or evidence of available additional brand demand, so none is inferred.
